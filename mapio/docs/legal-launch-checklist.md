# Legal & compliance launch checklist

All legal pages in the app are **drafts** and carry a visible banner. Nothing here is legal advice.

- [ ] Terms of Service drafted and approved by counsel. Fill every `[BRACKETED]` item: entity, address, governing law, liability, refund policy aligned with Paddle's buyer terms and statutory withdrawal rights.
- [ ] Privacy Policy approved. Covers:
  - processors and their regions
  - international transfers
  - retention schedule
  - location processing
  - trial-abuse email hash
  - Georgian Law on Personal Data Protection
  - GDPR, if EU users are targeted
- [ ] Cookie notice / consent, needed if any non-essential analytics are added. Only essential cookies are used today.
- [ ] Trial and renewal disclosures reviewed: price, 7-day length, first-charge timing, auto-renewal, how to cancel. The checkout, pricing page, and billing page show them, and Paddle sends its own emails.
- [ ] Brand review:
  - no Tesla logos or trade dress
  - "Tesla" used only descriptively, with the independence disclaimer on the landing page, footer, FAQ, Terms, and Privacy
  - MAPIO name and trademark availability checked in target markets
- [ ] Road-safety disclaimer is present (footer, Terms, How it works). Consider showing it once before first guidance.
- [ ] Map and data attribution visible: OSM/MapTiler on the map, Geoapify, Open-Meteo, NBG, Open Charge Map with per-record licences.
- [ ] Provider terms reviewed for commercial use, caching, and storage: MapTiler, Geoapify, Open-Meteo, OCM, NBG.
- [ ] Paddle seller verification complete; refund and cancellation policy URLs registered with Paddle.
- [ ] Support contact and response-time commitment published (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL`).
- [ ] Data subject request process: export and delete are self-serve in Account; there's a documented manual path.
- [ ] Accessibility statement (optional).
